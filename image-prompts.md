# Authentic photography prompts (future asset production)

Use real commissioned photography or carefully reviewed generated imagery only where a people-first campaign needs it. Do not substitute a synthetic employee face in product UI.

1. **Small-business owner reviewing call outcomes** — Indian business owner in a bright Bengaluru office, seated at a tidy desk, laptop seen at a soft three-quarter angle, warm morning side light, muted forest-green and coral objects in frame, documentary editorial style, generous negative space on the left for copy, 3:2 landscape.
2. **Team handoff moment** — two Indian operations teammates discussing a mobile call summary, over-shoulder composition, natural window light, hands and devices only if anatomically credible, no posed smiles, tactile paper notes and a plant, understated modern startup editorial, 4:3 landscape.
3. **Field sales follow-up** — Indian property advisor outside a contemporary apartment building reviewing an incoming WhatsApp confirmation, late-afternoon soft light, candid motion, no visible third-party logo, lots of clean background, 9:16 vertical.
